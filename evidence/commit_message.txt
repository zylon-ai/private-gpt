fix(tools): bound tree expansion by the tightest applicable token limit

calculate_maximum_token_expansion() returned the widest of the three limits it
is given instead of the tightest, and never compared the result against the
model's context window. With a configured chat.maximum_context_length of 2048
and an 8192-token model, a None token_limit yielded 8192 (4x the configured
context budget), and a 4096 token_limit could yield 8192 as well because
maximum_context_length was applied as a floor rather than a cap. The budget is
the cap the tree-expansion post-processor enforces, so retrieved context grew
past both the operator's setting and the model window.

Return the minimum of the non-None limits, and add the missing coverage for the
helper (it had none).
